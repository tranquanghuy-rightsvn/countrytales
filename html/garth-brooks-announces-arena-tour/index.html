              GARTH BROOKS ANNOUNCES ARENA TOUR

              • 30/07/2026

              GARTH BROOKS ANNOUNCES ARENA TOUR: Country music touring legend Garth Brooks is setting out on a familiar adventure: launching a new arena tour.

              Brooks announced the Blame It All On My Roots Tour Tuesday after he began his career selling out arenas across the country.

              The tour will mark the return of the iconic drum pod, which was a highlight of Brooks’ performances in the 90s.

              “Going back into the arenas is about putting the stadium show in a box,” said Brooks in a release. “The excitement gets multiplied by the intimacy. Every seat is a great seat. This is personal.”
